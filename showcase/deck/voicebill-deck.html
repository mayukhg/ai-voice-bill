Product case study

VoiceBill

Voice-first bill reminders and OTP-secured payments — scoped, demoed, and roadmapped as a PM portfolio piece.

Role: Product Manager · Domain: Consumer fintech / smart home · Partner stack: Flask + AWS

01 · Problem

People don’t forget bills because paying is hard. They forget because reminders are easy to ignore.

  • App push and email compete with dozens of other alerts
  • Late fees are high-cost, low-drama failures
  • Paying still means unlocking a phone and hunting the right biller

Opportunity: meet the user at the speaker with a short, secure path to “paid.”

02 · Who & JTBD

Primary user

Maya, 34 — Echo in the kitchen, pays online, still misses 1–2 bills/year.

JTBD: When I’m busy at home, tell me what’s due and let me settle it without five apps.

Secondary: accessibility / hands-free users.

Buyer: billers/banks cutting “I forgot” support cost.

03 · Insight

Convenience without a second factor is a non-starter. OTP is the trust bargain.

  • Voice lists and initiates; money moves only after SMS OTP
  • Matches bank mental models; keeps skill risk narrative credible
  • Voice biometrics explicitly out of scope for v1

04 · Solution

Two flows. One product.

Flow 1 · Reminders

“What’s due?” → bills in 7 days → spoken summary → offer to pay.

Flow 2 · Pay + OTP

“Pay utility” → amount → SMS OTP → speak code → mock pay → receipt.

07 · Security

MFA before money. Mock rails on purpose.

  • 6-digit OTP · 5-minute expiry · single-use
  • Gateway mocked — proves UX/risk without PCI scope
  • OAuth account linking on Next, not claimed as done

08 · Architecture (honest)

What exists vs roadmap

Flask API · reminders, bills, pay, Lex webhook
DynamoDB · Polly · SNS wrappers
Lex fulfillment (session gap to fix)
Alexa skill / ASK · OAuth · proactive events

10 · Metrics

North star: paid-by-due-date rate

Reminder→Pay start→OTP ok→Paid
  • Target: −40% missed payments vs control
  • Reminder → pay ≥ 25% · OTP success ≥ 90% · session < 2 min

11 · Risks

What could kill this

  • Skill cert / linking — start OAuth early
  • OTP latency — set expectation in voice prompt
  • Misheard IDs — prefer bill type slots in v1
  • Shoulder-surfing — OTP mandatory + amount read-back
