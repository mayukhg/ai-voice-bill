Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Bill Payment Assistant ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Alexa skill and related services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (user ID, email, phone number)
  • Bill information (bill types, amounts, due dates)
  • Payment information (processed through secure payment gateways)
  • Voice commands and interactions with the skill

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information and Alexa device identifiers
  • Usage data and interaction logs
  • Error logs and diagnostic information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide bill management and payment services
  • Send bill reminders and notifications
  • Process payments securely
  • Authenticate and verify your identity
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) to store and process your data. All data is:

  • Encrypted at rest using AWS encryption
  • Transmitted over secure HTTPS/TLS connections
  • Stored in secure AWS DynamoDB databases
  • Protected by AWS security best practices

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • AWS Services: DynamoDB, Lambda, SNS, Polly, Lex (data storage and processing)
  • Amazon Alexa: Voice interface and skill platform
  • Payment Processors: Secure payment gateway services (no card data stored)

6. Account Linking

When you link your account, we use OAuth 2.0 to securely authenticate you. We store your user ID and necessary account information to provide the service. You can unlink your account at any time through the Alexa app.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of certain data processing
  • Unlink your account

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@your-domain.com

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Payment records are retained for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
